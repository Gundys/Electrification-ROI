When To Sell Electric

✓ Ideal Fit

  • Coating-only projects, tank relines, water/wastewater, roofing, waterproofing
  • Contractors who own or rent 375 CFM or larger compressors
  • Paint shops with centralized shop air or aging compressors
  • Contractors focused on reducing fuel and operating costs

! Qualify Carefully

  • Jobs requiring blasting and priming: The blast and prime phase will still require a compressor, but subsequent coating applications can go electric — allowing the compressor to be returned, redeployed, or shut down once blasting is complete.
  • Buyers focused only on purchase price: Reframe the conversation around total Cost of Ownership (COO) — fuel, maintenance, rental, transport, downtime, and air-system support all add up over the life of the equipment.
  • Customers unfamiliar with electric (offer a demo)

× Stay With Air-Powered

  • Hazardous-location (HazLoc) applications — with one exception: the King E-Series is the only electric sprayer that IS HazLoc certified. For HazLoc jobs outside the King E-Series, air-powered remains the recommended solution.
